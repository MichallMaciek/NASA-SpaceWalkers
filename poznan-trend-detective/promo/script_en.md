# English narration: 30-second submission cut

Voice: ElevenLabs, model `eleven_v4`, voice "George - Warm, Captivating Storyteller" (details in `README.md`).
The words follow `docs/VIDEO_30S.md` (66 spoken words). 30,000 zł and 1,000,000 zł are the team's illustrative hook, not data.

Format, read by `tts.py` and `build.py`:
each `## <scene>` section is one scene of the film, each `- ` line is one subtitle and one piece of the voice-over.
After `||` comes the text the voice reads when it differs from the subtitle.
`build.py` re-checks every number against the data (CHECKS section) on each build.

## hook
- A used car gets a full inspection.
- A million-złoty house gets nice photos.

## growth
- Around Poznań, built-up land more than doubled between 1990 and 2020.

## inspect
- Type any address and get a technical inspection of the land:
- building, trees, heat, groundwater, radar.

## nisar
- NASA MODIS, Landsat and NISAR data.

## forecast
- Every trend tested, the forecast checked on years it never saw.

## close
- Poznań Trend Detective. Check the land before you sign.
